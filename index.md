## Research Interests

I work at the intersection of bio-inspired security and machine learning for science.

- <span class="highlight"><b><u>Artificial Immune Systems & Intrusion Detection</u></b></span>: LLM-guided antibody generation, immune-tolerance filtering, and few-shot detection of unknown attacks in industrial control systems.
- <span class="highlight"><b><u>Noisy-Label & Configuration Learning</u></b></span>: multi-resolution community configurations as label-free structural features for downstream prediction.
- <span class="highlight"><b><u>Machine Learning for Science</u></b></span>: physiologically-constrained sleep staging, ECG–electrolyte inference, biomedical signal modeling, graph & matrix visualization.
