We propose a brain-inspired perspective on *configurations*—valid hierarchical clusterings produced via community detection at varying resolutions—arguing that they offer a faithful computational analog of early-stage cognitive grouping. We characterize configurations as a label-free similarity substrate, contrast them with register-token behaviors in Vision Transformers, and discuss implications for unsupervised representation learning.
