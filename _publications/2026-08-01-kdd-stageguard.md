We present **StageGuard**, a sleep-staging framework that injects physiological prior knowledge into the model so that predicted hypnograms respect clinically plausible stage-transition dynamics. The result is improved accuracy and substantially more reliable, interpretable hypnograms compared with unconstrained deep baselines.
